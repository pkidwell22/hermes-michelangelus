## Painting

Nicolas Poussin, *The Arcadian Shepherds / Et in Arcadia ego* (c. 1638), Louvre, INV 7300 / MR 2339. Poussin died in 1665; the underlying painting is public domain.

The current theme's faithful reproduction is embedded as an offline JPEG data URL. Its recorded byte-source is:
https://mountainmeadowsystems.com/assets/art/arcadia.jpg

Public-domain artwork/reproduction context (not a claim that these are identical image bytes):
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Poussin_-_Et_in_Arcadia_Ego_-_Louvre_INV_7300
