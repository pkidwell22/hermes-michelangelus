## Painting

Caspar David Friedrich, *Winter Landscape with Church* (Dortmund version), 1811, oil on canvas, 33 × 45 cm, Museum für Kunst und Kulturgeschichte der Stadt Dortmund, inv. 4737. Friedrich died in 1840; the underlying painting is public domain.

The current theme embeds the maintainer's copy of that painting as an offline JPEG data URL. The embedded bytes are not claimed to be identical to any single upload; the public-domain source reference used for identification is Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caspar_David_Friedrich_-_Winterlandschaft_mit_Kirche_(Dortmund).jpg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Winter_Landscape_with_church_(Dortmund)

The painting exists in a related version at the National Gallery, London; the Dortmund canvas is the one reproduced here. The artwork and the public-domain reproduction status are the subject of this notice; the museum's own photography may carry separate terms and is not redistributed as a file.

Earlier releases of this package embedded Nicolas Poussin, *The Arcadian Shepherds / Et in Arcadia ego* (c. 1638), Louvre, INV 7300 / MR 2339, also public domain, from `https://mountainmeadowsystems.com/assets/art/arcadia.jpg`. That notice remains accurate for those releases.

Faithful reproductions of two-dimensional public-domain art are treated as public domain in the United States; treatment can vary by jurisdiction. No exclusive rights are claimed over the painting. The package makes no image request at runtime.

## Companion plugins

The Saved artifacts and Work browser library companions contain no saved artifacts, catalogue entries, bookmarks, browsing history or research notes. They read only the recipient's own local `catalogue.json`, or a Chrome export the recipient selects, and keep derived entries in the app's local storage. The catalogue reader renders recipient pages in a sandboxed frame; no third-party page or script is bundled.
